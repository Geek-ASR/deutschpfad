A1 Mock Exam

Two full practice sittings in the Goethe/telc "Start Deutsch 1" format — Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, and Sprechen — built only from vocabulary and grammar the A1 units and the vocabulary bank already cover. Pick one below.

Finishing the timed sections saves your score locally and schedules missed questions for spaced review — see your dashboard or the privacy approach. Nothing is sent anywhere; nothing here is an official score.

Before you start

  • Hören (Listening) — 10 questions, ~10 minutes. Every audio question has a Play button; the German is never shown as text.
  • Lesen (Reading) — 10 questions, ~12 minutes. Short notices, a personal text, and two signs.
  • Schreiben (Writing) — Part 1 (fill in a form, ~5 minutes, auto-checked) and Part 2 (write a short message, self-checked against a model answer — there's no backend here to grade open writing).
  • Sprechen (Speaking) — three real exam formats to practice out loud, with links to more spoken practice. Also not machine-graded — that needs a partner.

Each timed section shows a countdown but won't cut you off — running past zero just means slower than a real exam would want, not a lost attempt. Questions show no right/wrong feedback until a section is done, the same way a real exam works.
