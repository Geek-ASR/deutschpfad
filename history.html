History

German History, in German

Seven milestones, from the Holy Roman Empire to reunification. Each opens to a plain-German version first — click "Detailed" for a more advanced account of the same event.

This is a compressed overview, not a substitute for a history course — seven events across more than a thousand years, chosen as commonly-taught milestones. The 1933–1945 entry covers the Nazi dictatorship and the Holocaust factually and briefly; it is presented seriously, not for shock value.
