B1 · Unit 4

Purpose Clauses

Goal: I can say what something is for with um … zu and damit, knowing which one the subjects allow, and use ohne … zu and (an)statt … zu for what didn't happen or happened instead.

Step 1 · Discover

Two ways to say "so that"

Same purpose — one with an infinitive, one with a full clause.

    Step 2 · Understand

    The subject decides

    One test settles which purpose structure to use: do both halves have the same subject?

    Same subject → um … zu + Infinitiv. No subject in the um-part; the Infinitiv goes last: Ich lerne Deutsch, um hier zu arbeiten. (I learn / I work — one person.)

    Different subjects → damit + Nebensatz. Subordinating, so the verb goes to the end: Ich spreche langsam, damit mich alle verstehen. (I speak / they understand — two subjects.) damit is always allowed; um … zu is not, when the subjects differ.

    zu and separable verbs. The zu slots inside the verb, written as one word: um mitzukommen, um aufzustehen. Normal and -ieren verbs keep it in front: um zu verstehen, um zu telefonieren.

    The same pattern, other meanings. ohne … zu = "without doing" (Er ging, ohne sich zu verabschieden); (an)statt … zu = "instead of doing" (Statt zu lernen, hat er ferngesehen). Different subject → the dass-versions: ohne dass, anstatt dass.

    Plain zu + Infinitiv. After many verbs and expressions — vorhaben, versuchen, vergessen, anfangen, sich freuen, es ist wichtig …: Ich habe vor, die Prüfung zu machen.
