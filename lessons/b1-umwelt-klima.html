B1 · Unit 16

Environment & Climate

Goal: I can talk about climate change and its causes, describe Germany's Energiewende, discuss what people can do about it, and understand a news report on the environment.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond recycling

    You already know Mülltrennung and Umweltschutz (A2). This unit is about discussing the bigger picture — policy, causes, and consequences.

    The big picture. Treibhausgase trap heat in the atmosphere, driving the Erderwärmung — the CO2-Ausstoß of cars, factories, and power plants is the number everyone argues about reducing.

    Germany's answer. The Energiewende is the shift away from fossile Brennstoffe toward erneuerbare Energien like Windkraft and Solarenergie — the Kohleausstieg is one visible piece of it.

    What you can do. This topic runs on a small set of verbs: umsteigen auf something greener, verzichten auf something wasteful, reduzieren your Fußabdruck, or sich engagieren für a cause.

    The stakes. Dürren, Überschwemmungen, and other Extremwetter are the visible consequences — along with a rising Meeresspiegel and threatened Artenvielfalt, as species aussterben faster than they're discovered.
