    Step 2 · Understand

    The same preposition, two cases

    Nine prepositions — in, an, auf, über, unter, vor, hinter, neben, zwischen — can take either the Dativ or the Akkusativ, and the choice carries meaning. In A1's Pronouns & Cases unit you met the cases; here's the one rule that decides between them:

    A location → Dativ. If the phrase answers wo? ("where?") — something is simply at a place, not moving — use the Dativ: Das Buch liegt auf dem Tisch ("on the table").

    Movement toward → Akkusativ. If the phrase answers wohin? ("where to?") — something is moving onto or into a place — use the Akkusativ: Ich lege das Buch auf den Tisch ("onto the table").

    The verb usually tells you which. Position verbs — liegen, stehen, sitzen, sein, hängen — go with the Dativ. Their motion partners — legen, stellen, setzen, gehen, fahren — go with the Akkusativ. Die Flasche steht im Kühlschrank vs. Ich stelle die Flasche in den Kühlschrank.

    Fused forms. in dem → im, in das → ins, an dem → am, an das → ans. So "in the fridge" (location) is im Kühlschrank, but "into the cinema" (movement) is ins Kino.

      Where this shows up next
