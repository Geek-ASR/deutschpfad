Migration & Integration

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond B1's paperwork vocabulary

    B1 Unit 25 gave you the bureaucratic process — Ausländerbehörde, Aufenthaltstitel, Widerspruch. This unit is about the social and political conversation around migration instead.

    Flüchtling is a specific category. der Flüchtling implies fleeing danger — narrower than the general Migrant or the neutral, official Zuwanderer.

    Contested terms deserve care. die Leitkultur and die Parallelgesellschaft are genuinely disputed in German public debate — worth recognising when you read or hear them, and worth using carefully.

    Integration vs. Eingliederung. B1 Unit 21 already taught die Integration — this unit's die Eingliederung is a more formal, often labour-market-specific near-synonym.

    Citizenship is its own path. die Einbürgerung — becoming a citizen — and die doppelte Staatsbürgerschaft now possible under recent reforms, sit at the legal endpoint of many people's story.
