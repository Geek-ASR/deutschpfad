    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond the basics of applying

    You already know how to apply for a job (A2). This unit is what happens once you have one.

    Contract types. Vollzeit vs. Teilzeit (hours); befristet vs. unbefristet (duration). A Probezeit usually comes first, then a shorter Kündigungsfrist applies until it ends.

    Flexible work. Homeoffice (working from home) and Gleitzeit (a flexible start/end time within limits) are common perks to ask about. Überstunden (overtime) are usually either paid extra or taken back as time off.

    Leaving well. Every employee is entitled to an Arbeitszeugnis — a formal written reference — when a job ends. A fristlose Kündigung (no notice) is rare and needs serious cause, usually after an Abmahnung (formal warning).

    Growing in a job. Weiterbildung (further training) feeds Aufstieg (promotion, "befördert werden"). Both often come up in the annual Mitarbeitergespräch, alongside a Gehaltsverhandlung if you ask for one.

    Job-ad adjectives. teamfähig, belastbar, eigenverantwortlich — the words almost every Anforderungsprofil uses. zuständig (whose job it is) and verantwortlich (accountable for the result) are close but not identical.
