Family & Care

Goal: I can discuss parental leave and childcare, talk about modern family structures, and explain how families care for both children and aging relatives.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond kinship words

    You already know Vater/Mutter/Geschwister (A1) and relationship verbs like sich verlieben (A2). This unit is about family as a social topic.

    Raising a family. Die Erziehung starts at home, but die Elternzeit and das Elterngeld support parents early on, and die Kinderbetreuung — often at a Kita — later.

    Juggling work and family. Die Vereinbarkeit von Familie und Beruf and sharing der Haushalt are constant themes in this discussion.

    Modern family shapes. Many children wachsen today in a Patchwork-Familie auf, alongside Angehörige who may be verwandt in less traditional ways.

    Caring the other direction. When a parent becomes pflegebedürftig, adult children often take over die Pflege — sometimes at home, sometimes via Altenpflege or a Pflegeheim.

    The bigger picture. Der Generationenvertrag, das Sorgerecht, and der Unterhalt are the legal and societal side of family life.
