    Step 2 · Understand

    How the system is organised

    You already know basic illness vocabulary (A2). This is how the system around it actually works.

    Insurance is mandatory. Versicherungspflicht applies to everyone. Most people are in the gesetzliche Krankenversicherung (a Krankenkasse you can choose); the private Krankenversicherung is mainly for the self-employed, civil servants, and high earners.

    The GP is the gateway. Your Hausarzt treats most things and gives you an Überweisung when you need a Facharzt. Always bring your Versichertenkarte.

    Small costs, not big bills. A Zuzahlung (like the Rezeptgebühr) is usually a small fixed amount — most GP visits themselves cost nothing at the point of care. Vorsorgeuntersuchungen and many Impfungen are free.

    Emergency vs. urgent. 112 (Notruf) or the Notaufnahme for genuine emergencies; 116117 (der ärztliche Notdienst) for something urgent outside office hours that isn't life-threatening.

    Beyond a single visit. stationär (overnight) vs. ambulant (same-day); Reha after surgery or serious illness; Pflegeversicherung for long-term care; Psychotherapie is covered too, though waits can be long.
