Housing & the Nebenkostenabrechnung

Goal: I can understand my annual Nebenkostenabrechnung, report a Mangel, understand a Mieterhöhung, and know my rights as a tenant in Germany.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond move-in day

    You already know the basics of renting (A2). This unit is about living in the flat — and knowing your rights.

    The annual reckoning. Your monthly Vorauszahlung is only an estimate. Once a year, the Nebenkostenabrechnung compares it to the real Betriebskosten — you either get a Guthaben back or owe a Nachzahlung.

    When something's broken. A serious Mangel (mould, broken heating) is reported with a written Mängelanzeige, with a Frist for repair — and can justify a Mietminderung, a legal right to pay less rent until it's fixed.

    Rent isn't free to raise. A Mieterhöhung has to be justified, usually against the Mietspiegel — the ortsübliche Vergleichsmiete for similar flats nearby.

    Ending a lease. As a tenant, you can kündigen with (usually) three months' notice. A landlord's options are much narrower — Eigenbedarf is the most common legal ground.

    You're not alone in this. A Mieterverein reviews your Abrechnung, your Kündigung, and your rights for a small membership fee — the Mieterschutz in Germany is genuinely strong, but you have to know to use it.
