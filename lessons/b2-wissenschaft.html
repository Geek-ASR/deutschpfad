    Step 2 · Understand

    Near-synonyms that aren't quite synonyms

    This topic is full of words that look interchangeable in a dictionary but aren't in practice.

    Forschung vs. Studie. die Forschung is the general field; die Studie is one specific research project within it.

    Experiment vs. Versuch. das Experiment is a formal, controlled test; der Versuch is broader — it can mean the same thing, or just "an attempt" in everyday German.

    Beleg vs. B1's Nachweis. der Beleg is a specific piece of evidence supporting a claim; B1 Unit 25's der Nachweis is a more official, often bureaucratic proof document.

    Three stages, three words. B1's die Hypothese (an untested assumption) becomes B2 Unit 10's die These (a claim being argued for), which — if it holds up — becomes this unit's die Erkenntnis (an established finding).

    Grundlagenforschung vs. angewandte Forschung. Basic research has no immediate practical goal; applied research is aimed at a specific use — a distinction that comes up constantly in funding debates.
