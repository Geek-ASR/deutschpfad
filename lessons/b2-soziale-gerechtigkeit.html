Social Justice & Inequality

Goal: I can discuss economic inequality — wealth, income, poverty, and the systems meant to address them — as its own topic, not folded into discrimination or education.

    Step 2 · Understand

    A third angle on fairness

    B2 Unit 16 covered individual discrimination and prejudice; Unit 17 covered educational equity specifically. This unit adds the economic and structural layer.

    Wealth and income aren't the same thing. die Vermögensungleichheit measures accumulated assets; die Einkommensungleichheit measures ongoing earnings — different problems, different fixes.

    Gerechtigkeit vs. Gleichheit. die Chancengleichheit (B2 Unit 17) means identical treatment; this unit's die Chancengerechtigkeit means accounting for different starting points to reach a genuinely fair outcome.

    Structural, not personal. die strukturelle Benachteiligung is built into a system itself — different from B2 Unit 16's die Diskriminierung, which usually implies an individual act.

    Class, not just ethnicity. der Klassismus is discrimination based on social class — a distinct axis from B2 Unit 16's der Rassismus.
