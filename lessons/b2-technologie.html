    Step 2 · Understand

    A third angle on innovation

    B2 Unit 12 gave you innovation from a research angle — die Studie, das Patent. Unit 15 gave you AI and digital policy. This unit adds the business and entrepreneurship layer.

    Inkubator vs. Accelerator. der Inkubator supports very early ideas over a longer stretch of time; der Accelerator is faster and more structured, usually just a few months.

    Failure is part of the process. die Fehlerkultur — whether scheitern is treated as shameful or as normal, useful learning — shapes how a whole startup scene actually works.

    Old words, new frontiers. die Kryptowährung connects to B2 Unit 14's die Währung; der Börsengang connects to Unit 14's die Börse.

    Not the same as künstliche Intelligenz. die Robotik and der Quantencomputer are their own fields — they overlap with B2 Unit 15's AI in practice, but aren't the same technology.
