Conditional Inversion & Formal Connectors

Goal: I can state a condition without "wenn" — by inverting the verb, or with a formal connector like sofern, es sei denn, or im Falle — the way contracts, official notices, and formal writing do.

    Step 2 · Understand

    The same conditions, a more formal shape

    B1 taught wenn-clauses, including with Konjunktiv II. This unit doesn't add a new meaning — it adds two new, more formal ways to say the same thing: inversion, and a set of dedicated connectors.

    Drop wenn, invert the verb. Wenn es regnet, … becomes Regnet es, … The main clause keeps its normal verb-second word order.

    Works with Konjunktiv II too. Wäre ich reich, würde ich um die Welt reisen. / Hätte ich das gewusst, wäre ich nicht gekommen.

    sollte adds a formal, tentative future. Sollte es regnen, bleiben wir zu Hause. — more formal and slightly less certain than the plain-present inversion.

    Or use a dedicated connector. sofern (provided that), es sei denn (unless), im Falle (in the event of), and vorbehaltlich (subject to) all state a condition the way a contract or official notice would.

    Comma, always. The same rule as a fronted wenn-clause: a comma always separates the inverted condition from the main clause that follows.
