    Step 2 · Understand

    Two angles on education

    B1 Unit 8 gave you the vocabulary to navigate the German system yourself — Anerkennung, Immatrikulation, ECTS. This unit gives you the vocabulary to discuss and compare education systems as a topic.

    Equity has its own vocabulary. die Bildungsgerechtigkeit and die Chancengleichheit name the goal; die Bildungsungleichheit names the problem they're meant to fix.

    Teaching methods get debated too. der Frontalunterricht (lecture-style) versus handlungsorientierter Unterricht (learning by doing) is a recurring pedagogical argument.

    A specifically German contrast. Unlike some countries, Germany has no strong tradition of Eliteuniversitäten — most universities are seen as roughly comparable in standing.

    The PISA-Studie looms over all of it. The OECD's international comparison regularly makes headlines and reshapes German Bildungspolitik debates.
