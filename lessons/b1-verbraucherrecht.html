    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond shopping

    You already know reklamieren and umtauschen from A2's banking unit. This unit is about contracts, warranties, and knowing your rights.

    Before you sign. Every Vertrag comes with die AGB and das Kleingedruckte — once you sign, it becomes verbindlich.

    Buying online. Most distance purchases come with a fourteen-day Widerrufsrecht — you can widerrufen without giving a reason.

    When something breaks. Die Garantie is a voluntary promise from the manufacturer; die Gewährleistung is the seller's legal obligation, required by law — the two are easy to mix up.

    Cancelling something. Check the Kündigungsfrist before you kündigen — a fristlose Kündigung is only allowed for serious cause.

    When things go wrong. If a product is mangelhaft, you can file a Reklamation and demand an Erstattung — and die Verbraucherzentrale can help for free if a company won't cooperate.
