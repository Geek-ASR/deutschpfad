    Step 2 · Understand

    Why German verbs keep changing their ending

    In English, a verb barely changes: I speak, you speak, we speak — only "he/she speaks" gets an extra letter. German verbs change their ending for every subject, and the endings follow a real pattern rather than being random. This unit's pattern is the one you'll use constantly: for ich ("I"), a regular verb's ending is almost always -e.

    kommen (to come) → drop -en, add -e → ich komme

    That's the whole rule for regular verbs. The next step lets you test it on every verb in this unit.
