A2 · Unit 16

Adjective Endings with No Article

Goal: when there's no article, I can give the adjective the ending der/die/das would have carried — guter Kaffee, kaltes Wasser, mit heißem Tee.

Step 1 · Discover

Drop the article

Before any rules — the Unit 12 phrase, then the same thing with no article at all. The ending jumps onto the adjective.

    Step 2 · Understand

    The adjective becomes the article

    The third and last pattern. Same predicate / attributive split as before. Here there is no article — so nothing is showing the case and gender except the adjective, and it takes the job on: it gets the ending der / die / das / dem / den would have had.

    guter Kaffee (like der), frische Milch (like die), kaltes Wasser (like das), nette Leute (like die plural), mit heißem Tee (like dem). The endings are just der/die/das minus the d-: -er, -e, -es, -en, -em, -er.

    m f n pl
    Nomguter Weinfrische Milchkaltes Wassernette Leute
    Akkguten Weinfrische Milchkaltes Wassernette Leute
    Datgutem Weinfrischer Milchkaltem Wassernetten Leuten
    Genguten Weinsfrischer Milchkalten Wassersnetter Leute

    The one twist: genitive masculine / neuter is -en, not the expected -es — the noun's own -s already marks it (ein Glas guten Weins). Rare in speech; just recognise it.

    Where you meet no article: uncountable / mass nouns (frisches Brot, heißer Tee), bare plurals (deutsche Autos, kleine Kinder), after viel, etwas, wenig (they don't decline — viel frische Luft), on menus and in recipes (gebratener Fisch mit Reis), and in fixed phrases: vielen Dank, herzlichen Glückwunsch, guten Appetit, schönes Wochenende — all article-less accusative.
