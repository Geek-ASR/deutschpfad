Step 1 · Discover

The same idea, two ways

One reason, said with a subordinating connector and then with an adverb.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Three groups — and each moves the verb differently

    The meaning ("because", "although") is the easy part. The word order is what B1 exams test.

    1 · Subordinating conjunctions — weil, da, obwohl, während, sodass. They send the verb to the end of their clause, and the clause is set off with a comma: …, weil ich keine Zeit habe.

    2 · Coordinating conjunctions — denn (and und, aber, oder, sondern). They sit in "position zero" and change nothing: subject, then verb, as normal: …, denn ich habe keine Zeit.

    3 · Conjunctional adverbs — deshalb, deswegen, daher, darum, trotzdem, dennoch, folglich. They take position 1, so the verb stays second and the subject comes after it: Ich habe keine Zeit, deshalb komme ich nicht.

    Two that hide in the middle. nämlich (reason) and allerdings (soft objection) can't start a clause — they stand just after the verb: Ich komme nicht; ich habe nämlich keine Zeit.

    Not connectors — prepositions. wegen + Genitiv (reason) and trotz + Genitiv (concession) take a noun, not a clause: trotz des Regens, wegen der Bauarbeiten.

      Step 9 · Encounter again

      Where this shows up next

      These connectors are the backbone of every B1 opinion text and discussion: state a view, back it with weil / denn, concede the other side with zwar … aber or obwohl, and close with deshalb. Unit 4 adds the purpose connectors damit and um … zu.
