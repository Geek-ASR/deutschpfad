B2 · Unit 4

lassen & sich lassen

Goal: I can say I'm having something done, say that something can be done using sich lassen, and stop confusing lassen with verlassen.

Step 1 · Discover

Two sentences, two completely different meanings

The kind of pair that trips learners up until it suddenly clicks.

    Step 2 · Understand

    One verb, several jobs

    You've used lassen since A2 for basic permission. This unit covers everything else it does.

    Causative: having something done. lassen conjugated + a second infinitive at the end: Ich lasse mir die Haare schneiden. You're not cutting your own hair — someone else is, and you're causing/allowing it.

    sich lassen: a passive alternative. Das Problem lässt sich lösen. = "The problem can be solved" — shorter than kann gelöst werden and extremely common in both speech and formal writing.

    Perfekt: a double infinitive. Just like a modal verb, lassen + another infinitive keeps both infinitives at the end in the Perfekt — never gelassen here: Ich habe mir die Haare schneiden lassen.

    Two fixed idioms. Lass mich in Ruhe. ("leave me alone") and Lass das! ("stop that!") — memorize these as fixed phrases rather than building new sentences from the pattern.

    The big trap: verlassen. verlassen means "to leave a place" — a completely different, inseparable verb that just happens to look related. Ich lasse das Fenster offen, aber ich verlasse jetzt das Haus. Nearby: hinterlassen (leave behind an object/message), zulassen (permit formally), unterlassen (refrain from, legal register), loslassen (let go of), and nachlassen (diminish, wane).
