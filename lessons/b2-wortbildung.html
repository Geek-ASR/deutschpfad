    Step 2 · Understand

    Five prefixes, five meanings

    You met noun-forming suffixes (-ung, -heit, -keit) in B1 Unit 10. This unit is about verb prefixes and adjective suffixes.

    ent- and er-: opposite-ish energies. ent- = a beginning or a removal (entdecken, entfernen); er- = a result you've achieved (erreichen, erfinden).

    zer- and ver-: things going wrong. zer- is the most reliable prefix in German — it always means breaking apart (zerbrechen, zerstören). ver- is broader, but very often marks a negative change (verlieren, verschwinden, sich verletzen).

    be-: makes a verb transitive. be- often absorbs a preposition: antworten auf → beantworten; steigen auf → besteigen. No more preposition, just a direct object.

    Negating: un- and miss-. un- is German's most productive negating prefix for adjectives (unmöglich, unbekannt); miss- does something similar for verbs (missverstehen).

    Adjective suffixes. -lich and -ig are the two workhorses (freundlich, mutig); -voll = "full of," -reich = "rich in," -los = "without" — hoffnungsvoll, erfolgreich, arbeitslos.
