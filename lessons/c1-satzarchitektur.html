Complex Sentence Architecture

    Step 2 · Understand

    Everything so far, working together

    Units 1–7 each taught one construction. Real C1 sentences combine several at once. This unit is the toolkit for handling that — as a reader taking a sentence apart, and as a writer building one up.

    The bracket still holds. However long a sentence gets, the finite verb stays in position 2 and its infinitives/participles stay at the end — the Satzklammer never breaks.

    Clauses nest inside clauses. Er sagte, dass er glaube, dass die Lösung, die sie vorgeschlagen hatten, funktionieren werde. Three layers, each verb-final in its own clause.

    Some elements move to the Nachfeld. Er ist schneller gelaufen, als wir erwartet hatten. A comparison or infinitive clause trails after the final verb, rather than crowding the middle field.

    Asides interrupt mid-flow. Das Projekt – so hieß es zumindest offiziell – sei termingerecht abgeschlossen worden. A parenthetical Einschub, marked by dashes.

    Find the core, then add layers back. Strip everything to subject–verb–object first; as a writer, do the reverse — start simple, add one layer at a time.
