B1 · Unit 8

Subjective Modal Verbs

Goal: I can express a guess with the modal verbs — from near-certain (muss) to hearsay (soll) to a doubted self-claim (will) — and guess about the past with muss … getan haben.

Step 1 · Discover

Same word, two very different jobs

müssen as a duty, and müssen as a guess.

    Step 2 · Understand

    A scale of certainty, and where the evidence comes from

    Modal verbs don't just express duty and ability — they also grade how sure you are.

    Objektiv vs. subjektiv. Sie muss um acht da sein (duty) vs. Sie muss noch im Stau stehen (I'm almost sure, based on the time). Same word, no form change — only context tells you which.

    The certainty scale. muss (near-certain) → dürfte (fairly confident) → kann/könnte (a real possibility) → mag (a rough estimate, or conceding a point). All + Infinitiv: Sie dürfte um die dreißig sein.

    Where the evidence comes from. soll = someone told you (hearsay): Der Film soll gut sein. will = the subject claims it about itself, and you have doubts: Er will nichts gewusst haben. wird (wohl) = your own expectation: Sie wird wohl noch schlafen.

    Guessing about the past. The modal stays in the present; the main verb becomes Infinitiv Perfekt (Partizip II + haben/sein), both at the very end: Er muss das Fenster offen gelassen haben. Never a Präteritum modal here.

    Plain adverbs say the same thing. wahrscheinlich/vermutlich ≈ dürfte; vielleicht ≈ kann/könnte; sicher/bestimmt ≈ muss; angeblich ≈ soll/will; offenbar/anscheinend = based on what you can see.

      Step 9 · Encounter again

      Where this shows up next

      soll as hearsay is everywhere in news reports and gossip; will as a doubted claim is standard in reporting an accusation. The Infinitiv Perfekt you just practised is the exact building block for the reported-speech Konjunktiv I of Unit 11.
