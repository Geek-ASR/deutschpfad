C1 · Unit 10

20th-Century German History

Goal: I can talk about the events that shaped modern Germany — the Weimar Republic, National Socialism, division, and reunification — with the vocabulary a C1 reading text simply assumes I already know.

Two dates, two turning points

Both sentences name a moment that changed the course of German history.

    Step 2 · Understand

    The shared background every C1 text assumes

    German news, literature, and essays constantly reference this history in passing, without explaining it. This unit is the background knowledge and vocabulary that makes those references make sense.

    From democracy to dictatorship. Weimarer Republik (1919–1933) → Nationalsozialismus (1933–1945) → Zweiter Weltkrieg and Holocaust.

    One country, then two. The Besatzungszonen hardened into the Teilung Deutschlands — DDR and BRD — for the duration of the Kalte Krieg.

    A wall goes up, and comes down. Mauerbau (1961) → Mauerfall (1989) → Wiedervereinigung (1990).

    Germany keeps working through it. Vergangenheitsbewältigung and Erinnerungskultur are ongoing, not finished — visible in every Gedenkstätte and Jahrestag.
