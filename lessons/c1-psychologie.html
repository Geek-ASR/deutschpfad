Psychology & Human Behaviour

Goal: I can name the mental processes behind behaviour — perception, motivation, cognitive bias, conditioning — that shape what people do without them always noticing why.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond character traits

    B1's persoenlichkeit unit gave you charakter words and conflict-resolution verbs for everyday interactions. This unit is psychology as a discipline — the mental processes underneath the behaviour.

    Behaviour has drivers. Trieb, Motivation, Impuls, and Antrieb each name a different kind of push toward action.

    The mind distorts systematically. A kognitive Verzerrung like Bestätigungsfehler or Herdenverhalten shapes judgment without the person noticing.

    Learning changes behaviour automatically. Konditionierung links a Reiz to a response until it becomes a Reflex.

    Some traits are given, some are built. angeboren vs. erlernt — and Prägung and Sozialisation explain how the "built" half happens.
