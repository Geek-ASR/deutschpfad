Mobility & Travel

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond booking a trip

    You already know fahren/fliegen/ankommen and asking for directions (A2). This unit is about commuting, delays, and getting around day to day.

    The system. Der ÖPNV splits into Nahverkehr (local) and Fernverkehr (long-distance) — a Verbindung may need one or more Anschlüsse, all listed in the Fahrplan.

    When it goes wrong. A Baustelle or a Stau often causes a Verspätung — sometimes a train fällt entirely aus, and there's an Ersatzverkehr instead.

    Know your rights. Fahrgastrechte entitle you to an Entschädigung after a long enough delay — worth knowing if you travel by Fernverkehr often.

    Booking and changing plans. You buchen a trip, can stornieren or umbuchen it — and the Deutschlandticket has made pendeln a lot cheaper nationwide.

    Beyond your own car. Carsharing and a Mitfahrgelegenheit are common, often more klimafreundlich alternatives — a nice callback to Unit 16.
