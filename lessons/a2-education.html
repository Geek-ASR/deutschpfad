    Step 2 · Understand

    A few distinctions worth getting right

    Mostly vocabulary — but four contrasts trip learners up.

    studieren vs. lernen. studieren = to be enrolled in a subject at university (Ich studiere Jura). lernen = to learn / to study for something specific (Ich lerne für die Klausur). Never "Ich studiere für die Klausur".

    Class types. Vorlesung — the professor talks, hundreds listen. Seminar — small group, you discuss and give Referate. Übung — you practise the material.

    Assessment types. Klausur — a written exam in a hall. Hausarbeit — a long text written at home over weeks (also means "housework"!). Referat — an oral presentation in a seminar. You bestehen (pass) or you fallen durch (fail — Perfekt with sein).

    Grades. At university: 1,0 is the best, 4,0 just passes, 5,0 is a fail. At school it's 1 (best) to 6 (worst) — the opposite feel from many countries, and different again from the university scale.
