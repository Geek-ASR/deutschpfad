Elevated Cohesion Devices for Essay Writing

Goal: I can hold a formal essay together with the connectors it actually needs — des Weiteren, zudem, mithin, demzufolge, zumal, vor diesem Hintergrund — instead of falling back on B1's und/aber/deshalb everywhere.

Step 1 · Discover

Two ways to add a point

Both sentences add a further point to an argument — but one sounds like a B1 essay, the other like a C1 one.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond und, aber, deshalb

    B1 and B2 built a solid connector toolkit — reasons, contrasts, two-part connectors. C1 essay writing adds a further layer: formal adverbs for adding points, drawing conclusions, and tying paragraphs back to earlier context.

    Adding a point, formally. des Weiteren, ferner, überdies, and zudem all add a further point — zudem is the most common and flexible of the four.

    Drawing a conclusion. mithin, demzufolge, and infolgedessen all say "therefore" — more formal than B1's deshalb/deswegen.

    zumal adds emphasis to a reason. Wir sollten früh losfahren, zumal am Freitag mit Stau zu rechnen ist. It behaves like weil or da — verb to the end.

    Tying back to context. vor diesem Hintergrund and in diesem Zusammenhang both link a new point back to something established earlier in the text.

    Position 1 or the Mittelfeld — your choice. Zudem ist der Vorschlag kostengünstig. / Der Vorschlag ist zudem kostengünstig. Both are correct; position 1 gives the connector more emphasis.

      Where this shows up next
