B2 · Unit 24

Intercultural Communication

Goal: I can recognise and discuss the mechanics of how communication actually differs across cultures — style, timing, body language, and etiquette.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Three earlier angles, one new one

    B2 Unit 11 introduced interkulturelle Kompetenz as a workplace skill. Unit 16 covered migration policy and contested cultural-debate terms. Unit 21 covered art's role in cultural representation. This unit gives you the mechanics of the conversation itself.

    Direct vs. indirect. direkte Kommunikation says what it means plainly; indirekte Kommunikation relies on context, hints, and tone.

    High-context vs. low-context. In a Hochkontextkultur, shared context carries as much meaning as the words; a Niedrigkontextkultur relies mainly on what's actually said.

    Time itself works differently. A monochrones Zeitverständnis treats schedules as fixed and sequential; a polychrones Zeitverständnis handles time more flexibly.

    Stereotype is the starting point, not the endpoint. das Stereotyp feeds B2 Unit 16's das Vorurteil — this unit's goal is staying vorurteilsfrei instead.
