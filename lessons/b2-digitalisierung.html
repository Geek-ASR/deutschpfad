    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond B1's digitalisation basics

    B1 Unit 17 gave you the everyday vocabulary — Plattform, Algorithmus, Filterblase, Datenschutz. This unit adds the policy and security layer on top.

    KI, Algorithmus, Automatisierung. B1's der Algorithmus is a fixed set of rules; künstliche Intelligenz can learn and adapt beyond fixed rules; this unit's die Automatisierung is using either to replace manual work.

    Überwachung vs. B1's Datenschutz. die Überwachung is the act of monitoring; B1's der Datenschutz is the legal right meant to limit it.

    Meinungsfreiheit online. B2 Unit 13's die Meinungsfreiheit gets its own digital-era tension here — weighed constantly against Hassrede and accusations of Zensur when platforms moderate content.

    One thread ties it together. die Verantwortung der Plattformen — how much a platform should answer for what its users post, including B1's die Falschmeldung — is the question underneath almost every term in this unit.
