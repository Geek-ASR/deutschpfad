Genitive Prepositions

Goal: I can use the prepositions that take the Genitive — wegen, aufgrund, trotz, während, innerhalb, laut, mithilfe and more — form the noun phrase correctly, and recognise them when they pile up in a letter from an office.

    Step 2 · Understand

    The form, the meaning groups, the register

    You met wegen / trotz / während / statt at A2. B1 adds the fuller set and the "written vs spoken" split.

    The form. preposition + a Genitive noun phrase: masc./neut. → des …(e)s (des Regens, des Verkehrs); fem./plural → der … (der Woche, der Kosten).

    By meaning. Reason: wegen, aufgrund, angesichts, infolge. Concession: trotz, ungeachtet. Time: während, innerhalb, außerhalb, anlässlich. Place: innerhalb, außerhalb, oberhalb, unterhalb, jenseits. Source / topic: laut, hinsichtlich, bezüglich, seitens. Means: mithilfe, anhand, mittels.

    Spoken German pulls them to the Dative. wegen dem Stau, trotz dem Regen, während dem Essen — common when speaking, still corrected in writing. With a pronoun it's almost always Dative: wegen dir, wegen mir.

    A bare noun stays uninflected. laut Gesetz, trotz Schnee, zwecks Anmeldung. And after a plural with no article, innerhalb switches to von + Dative: innerhalb von zwei Wochen.

    Where they live: officialese. A letter from a Behörde, a contract, a news report — aufgrund … hinsichtlich … seitens … mittels … stack up. You mainly need to read them; in your own writing, one per sentence is plenty.

      Where this shows up next

      These prepositions are all over the B1 Lesen part — notices, e-mails from the Amt, short news items. A couple (aufgrund, angesichts) are useful in your own opinion essay to open a sentence formally. The nominal style they belong to is the subject of Unit 13.
