    Step 2 · Understand

    The words that decide what you pay

    This unit is mostly vocabulary — but four words carry the money, so start there.

    Kaltmiete (KM) — the rent for the flat itself. Nebenkosten (NK) — heating, water, rubbish, caretaker, usually paid monthly in advance. Warmmiete (WM) = KM + NK, the number you actually transfer. Kaution — a deposit of two to three Kaltmieten, paid up front and returned (minus damage) when you move out.

    Ad shorthand. 2 ZKB = 2 Zimmer, Küche, Bad · EBK = Einbauküche (fitted kitchen stays) · provisionsfrei = no estate agent's fee · Altbau / Neubau = pre-~1950 / modern · Erstbezug = you're the first to move in · 60 m² = 60 Quadratmeter.

    The verbs. You (the tenant) mieten a flat; the owner vermietet it. You besichtigen it at a Besichtigung, then einziehen (move in — Perfekt with sein, Unit 7) and later ausziehen. The whole thing is die Wohnungssuche, and in a big city it's schwierig.

    Once you're in. The Mietvertrag and the Hausordnung (quiet hours, rubbish sorting) set the rules; the Hausmeister handles repairs — Sag dem Hausmeister Bescheid. Most flats come with a Keller and, if you're lucky, a Balkon.

      Step 9 · Encounter again

      Where this shows up next

      Writing a short message to a landlord — introducing yourself, asking for a viewing, listing what you earn — is a standard A2 and B1 writing task.
