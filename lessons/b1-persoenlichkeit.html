B1 · Unit 22

Personality & Conflict

Goal: I can describe someone's character, talk about a disagreement, and explain how a conflict got resolved.

The kind of thing you'd read describing a character.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond moods

    You already know feelings like aufgeregt and nervös, and relationship verbs like sich streiten mit (A2). This unit is about character and resolving disagreements.

    Describing personality. Der Charakter is made up of many Charaktereigenschaften — ehrgeizig, geduldig, or stur, to name a few.

    When personalities clash. A Konflikt or Streit often starts as a small Meinungsverschiedenheit.

    Getting past it. You can sich on a Kompromiss einigen, or simply nachgeben.

    When someone's hurt. A Vorwurf or Kritik can sting — how someone responds often says more about their Charakter than the conflict itself.

    Making peace. A Missverständnis needs to be geklärt; sometimes someone needs to vermitteln; and forgiveness means being willing to verzeihen.
