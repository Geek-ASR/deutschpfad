    Step 2 · Understand

    Asking politely — and one thing to notice

    A blunt Ich will einen Kaffee ("I want a coffee") sounds demanding in German, the way it would in English. Two everyday alternatives sound polite instead: Ich möchte... ("I would like...") and Ich hätte gern... ("I would like..." — literally "I would gladly have," reusing gern from the last unit).

    Notice: ein becomes einen when the drink is masculine — Ich möchte einen Kaffee (der Kaffee) — but stays ein for neuter drinks (ein Wasser, das Wasser) and becomes eine for feminine ones (eine Cola, die Cola). That shift is the accusative case peeking through — you'll learn it properly soon, but for now, just match the pattern: masculine drinks take einen, everything else keeps its normal form.
