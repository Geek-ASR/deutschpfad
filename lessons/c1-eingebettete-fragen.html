Embedded Questions & Nuanced Reporting Verbs

Goal: I can fold a question into a larger sentence without reporting it as speech — and choose the right verb for how a source presents a claim: conceding it, disputing it, hinting at it, or stating it as fact.

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond reported speech

    B1's indirekte Rede taught embedded ob/W-questions specifically for reporting what someone asked, with Konjunktiv I. C1 generalises this: any verb or expression that needs a question as its content can take one — in plain indicative, if it's your own uncertainty rather than a report.

    ob and W-words work the same way as always. Ich weiß nicht, ob er kommt. / Mir ist nicht klar, warum er das getan hat. Verb to the end, question word stays put.

    It can be the subject, not just the object. Ob das Projekt finanzierbar ist, bleibt fraglich. The whole question is what "bleibt fraglich."

    Or sit in apposition to "die Frage." Die Frage, ob das Projekt finanzierbar ist, bleibt offen.

    Mood is the giveaway. Er fragte, ob sie komme. reports someone's question (Konjunktiv I). Ob sie kommt, weiß ich nicht. states your own uncertainty (indicative).

    Choose your reporting verb deliberately. einräumen (concede), bestreiten (deny), andeuten (hint), unterstellen (insinuate), klarstellen (clarify), suggerieren (imply), konstatieren (state as fact) — each signals a different stance toward the claim.
