    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond WLAN and apps

    You already know the mechanics — WLAN, das Passwort, hochladen (A2). This unit is about discussing media and digital life as a topic.

    Getting the news. The Massenmedien — Zeitung, Radio, Fernsehen, and online media — produce the Berichterstattung we call die Nachrichten. Every claim traces back to a Quelle.

    Can you trust it? Not every source is glaubwürdig — a Falschmeldung can spread fast. Medienkompetenz means knowing how to überprüfen what you read.

    How platforms shape what you see. An Algorithmus on every Plattform decides what you see, which can beeinflussen your Meinungsbildung — critics call the result a Filterblase.

    Privacy in a digital world. Der Datenschutz (the legal concept) and die Privatsphäre (the personal one) are both constantly discussed alongside das Urheberrecht.

    Living with it. Der Medienkonsum and die Bildschirmzeit come up whenever people discuss being süchtig nach their phone — or just watching a Streamingdienst too much.
