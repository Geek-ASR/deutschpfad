    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond B1's Arbeitswelt basics

    B1 Unit 8 gave you the everyday vocabulary — job ads, contract types, notice periods, performance reviews. This unit goes further: career paths, workplace structure, and self-employment.

    Career paths aren't all the same shape. die Karriereleiter (climbing within one field), der Quereinstieg (switching in sideways, informally), and die Umschulung (a formal, often state-supported retraining program) describe three different routes.

    Workplace structure has its own vocabulary. der/die Vorgesetzte (your direct manager), die Führungskraft (anyone in a leadership role), and der Betriebsrat — a distinctly German institution, the elected staff who represent employee interests.

    Self-employment has its own path, too. sich selbstständig machen — deepens A2's adjective selbstständig into the act of taking that step. A Freiberufler is legally narrower than "freelancer" in English — a liberal profession like a doctor, journalist, or translator.

    What job ads ask for. Durchsetzungsvermögen, Verhandlungsgeschick, Eigeninitiative, and interkulturelle Kompetenz — the soft-skill vocabulary that shows up constantly under "Anforderungen" in a job posting.
