Health & Medical Ethics

    Step 2 · Understand

    Beyond B1's system navigation

    B1 Unit 8 gave you the vocabulary to navigate German healthcare as a patient — insurance, referrals, co-payments. This unit is about the ethical questions the system itself raises.

    Autonomy runs through everything. das Selbstbestimmungsrecht underlies die Patientenverfügung and die Patientenautonomie — a patient's right to decide for themselves.

    Pflegenotstand isn't Pflegeversicherung. B1's die Pflegeversicherung is the insurance system. This unit's der Pflegenotstand is the actual staffing and capacity crisis — a different problem entirely.

    A familiar pattern, applied to healthcare. der Ärztemangel follows the same shape as B2 Unit 11's der Fachkräftemangel and Unit 17's der Lehrermangel.

    Everything comes down to weighing things against each other. B2 Unit 3's abwägen is the verb for almost every case in this unit — benefit against risk, autonomy against dignity, one patient's needs against another's.
