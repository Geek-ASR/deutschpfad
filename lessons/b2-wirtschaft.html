    Step 2 · Understand

    Reading the economic indicators

    A handful of numbers get reported constantly — knowing what they mean unlocks most of the coverage.

    Konjunktur vs. Wachstum. die Konjunktur is the short-term economic cycle — up this quarter, down the next. das Wachstum is the longer trend underneath it.

    Zoll has two meanings. A2 taught der Zoll as the customs checkpoint you pass through when travelling. Here, it means a tariff — a tax on traded goods, as in die Strafzölle (punitive tariffs) you'll read about in trade-war coverage.

    Angebot und Nachfrage. The economic sense of das Angebot (supply) is broader than the everyday "a deal" — paired with die Nachfrage (demand), it's the most basic idea in economics.

    Die soziale Marktwirtschaft. Germany's own economic model — a market economy combined with a strong social safety net — comes up constantly in civics and economics discussions specifically about Germany.
