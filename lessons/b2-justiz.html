    Step 2 · Understand

    Two kinds of "appeal"

    B1 Unit 25 gave you der Widerspruch — an administrative objection to a Behörde's decision. This unit's appeals are judicial instead.

    Berufung has two unrelated meanings. B2 Unit 11's die Berufung is a vocation or calling. This unit's die Berufung is a court appeal — completely different words that just happen to look identical.

    Klage vs. Anklage. die Klage is a civil lawsuit between private parties; die Anklage is a criminal charge brought by die Staatsanwaltschaft.

    Berufung, then Revision. A case can move up through several Instanzen — first a Berufung re-examines the facts, then a Revision checks only whether the law was applied correctly.

    The highest court of all. das Bundesverfassungsgericht doesn't rule on individual guilt — it rules on whether a law itself complies with das Grundgesetz (B2 Unit 13).
