    Step 2 · Understand

    Two things: the bins, and the verbs

    Germany takes Mülltrennung seriously. Household waste is sorted at home into several bins, and the colour tells you which is which.

    Braun — Biomüll: food and garden waste. Blau — Altpapier: paper and cardboard. Gelb — Gelber Sack / gelbe Tonne: plastic and metal Verpackungen. Grau / Schwarz — Restmüll: everything else. Altglas goes to a separate Container, sorted by colour — but deposit bottles (Pfandflaschen) go back to the Pfandautomat for your money.

    The verbs are mostly revision. wegwerfen is separable (Wirf das nicht weg). vermeiden, verbrauchen, verschmutzen are inseparable (ver-) → no ge- in the participle. sparen pairs with Energie / Strom / Wasser, often in a um … zu clause (Unit 18): Licht aus, um Strom zu sparen.

    Adjectives. umweltfreundlich (opposite umweltschädlich), nachhaltig (sustainable), and bio — which stays uninflected: Bio-Milch, ein Bio-Apfel, never *bioe Milch.

    One false friend. das Klima is the long-term pattern; das Wetter is today. The Klimawandel is the change to the former, not the latter.
