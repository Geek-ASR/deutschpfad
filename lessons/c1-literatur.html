    Step 2 · Understand

    The vocabulary of literary analysis

    This unit's C1 grammar toolkit already lets you write dense, formal sentences. This is the first topic unit — the specific vocabulary for talking about literature with precision.

    Everything belongs to one of three genres. Epik (prose), Lyrik (poetry), and Dramatik (drama) — every specific form fits under one of these.

    Who tells the story shapes what you can know. An auktorialer Erzähler knows everything; an Ich-Erzähler or personaler Erzähler is limited to one point of view — and that view might even be unzuverlässig.

    Stilmittel do deliberate work. Metapher, Symbol, Motiv, and Ironie are all techniques a reader is expected to notice and name.

    Interpretation has its own verbs. interpretieren and deuten both mean roughly "to interpret" — deuten leans more suggestive and intuitive.
