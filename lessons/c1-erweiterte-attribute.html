Extended Attributes

    Step 2 · Understand

    Not new — just much deeper than B1

    B1 Unit 9 showed one simple example of this: der auf dem Tisch liegende Brief. This unit goes further in three ways B1 didn't: agents, stacked modifiers, and — genuinely new — the same trick with a plain adjective instead of a participle.

    An agent can slot in. der von der Regierung verabschiedete Gesetzentwurf — the von-phrase names who did it, exactly like the agent in a Vorgangspassiv sentence.

    Modifiers can stack. der vom Parlament nach langer Debatte verabschiedete Gesetzentwurf — an agent and a time phrase, both in front of the same participle.

    An adjective works the same way — genuinely new. die für die Umwelt äußerst schädliche Chemikalie — schädlich isn't a participle at all, but its complement and degree word still move in front of it.

    Even a genitive can be pulled in. der sich seines Talents durchaus bewusste Künstler — bewusst takes a genitive complement, and that genitive phrase moves into the attribute too.

    The word order rule that always holds. Artikel + Erweiterung(en) + Partizip/Adjektiv + Nomen — everything between the article and the noun's final modifying word belongs to the attribute.
