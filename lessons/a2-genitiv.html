The Genitiv

Goal: I can form the Genitiv (das Ende des Films, die Tasche der Frau), ask wessen?, and use wegen, während, trotz — while knowing the spoken von-shortcut.

Step 1 · Discover

Linking two nouns

Before any rules — two sentences. One shows possession, one gives a reason. Both use the same case.

    Step 2 · Understand

    The fourth case

    You've used the Nominativ, Akkusativ and Dativ since A1. The Genitiv is the last one — it links two nouns, usually "X of Y" or "Y's X", and it answers wessen? ("whose?").

    The possessing noun comes second. das Ende des Films ("the end of the film"), das Auto meiner Eltern ("my parents' car"). Articles: des for der/das nouns, der for die nouns and all plurals. ein-words match: meines, eines, keines (m/n) and meiner, einer, keiner (f/pl).

    Masculine and neuter nouns take an ending. Add -s to longer nouns (des Films, des Autos) and -es to one-syllable nouns or ones ending in -s, -ß, -z, -x (des Mannes, des Kindes, des Hauses). Feminine and plural nouns add nothing: der Frau, der Kinder. Any adjective takes -en: trotz des schlechten Wetters.

    Four prepositions take the Genitiv: wegen (because of), während (during), trotz (despite), (an)statt (instead of). wegen des Staus, während der Ferien, trotz der Kälte. Don't mix up the preposition trotz (+ Genitiv) with the adverb trotzdem ("nevertheless" — verb second, no Genitiv), the same trap as deshalb in Unit 4.

    How people actually talk. In conversation the possessive Genitiv is often replaced by von + Dativ: das Auto von meinem Vater. And wegen frequently takes the Dativ in speech (wegen dem Wetter). Both are understood everywhere — but a written exam wants the Genitiv. Proper names just add -s, no apostrophe: Annas Auto, Peters Handy.

      Step 9 · Encounter again

      Where this shows up next

      The Genitiv is heavily written German — newspaper headlines (das Ergebnis der Wahl), formal notices (während der Öffnungszeiten), set phrases (meiner Meinung nach). Getting wegen / während / trotz automatic now means one less thing to think about when B1 starts asking you to argue a point in writing.
